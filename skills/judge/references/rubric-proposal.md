# Proposal / offer rubric (70 points + 30 core)

Applies to: send-proposals, call-deck proposals, offers, retainer proposals, quotes that
were reframed into proposals. The judge reads as the buyer who has to defend the spend
internally.

| # | Category | Pts | 10/10 looks like | 0 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Their problem, their words** | 12 | Page one states the buyer's situation in terms they used on the call, with a number that shows it was heard (pipeline, reply rate, headcount, months). The buyer thinks "they got it". | Opens with "About us"; a generic pain statement that fits any company. |
| P2 | **Outcome before activity** | 14 | What changes for the buyer, quantified with a horizon ("first weekly report in week 5, 2 signals detectable across the whole list by week 2"). Activities are listed only as the way to get there. | A list of deliverables and hours; outcomes as adjectives ("improved", "optimised"). |
| P3 | **Price tied to result** | 12 | Price sits next to the outcome it buys, with the logic visible (per month, per phase, what stops if it stops). Options, if any, differ by outcome, not by hours. | A number at the end with no logic; three tiers that are the same thing in different sizes. |
| P4 | **Proof that fits** | 10 | One or two neutral cases with numbers that match THIS buyer's stage and problem. Confidential names withheld cleanly ("a 60-person MEP firm"). | A wall of logos; a case from a different industry; no numbers. |
| P5 | **Risk removed** | 10 | What the buyer must supply (owner, hours per week, access) and what happens if a phase gate is not passed. Adoption is defined ("implemented when"), not just delivery. | Silence on what the buyer has to do; no exit or pause terms. |
| P6 | **Decision path** | 12 | Ends with one action, a date, and what happens after yes. The buyer can forward it and a colleague understands it without the call. | "Let me know your thoughts"; a question as the close; no date. |

**Type hard fails:** a real client name as proof in a document the buyer may
forward; hours as the unit of value with no outcome; a price with no scope boundary; the
closing line is a question.

**Judge input:** the proposal (HTML/PDF/markdown), the buyer in one line (role, company size,
stage, what they said hurts), this rubric, core.md. NOT the call notes, NOT the pricing
reasoning.
