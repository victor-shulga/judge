# Creative / infographic / carousel rubric (70 points + 30 core)

Applies to: LinkedIn infographics (1080×1350), carousels, post covers, banners. The judge
sees the rendered image(s) at feed size and the post text it will ship with, if any.

| # | Category | Pts | 10/10 looks like | 0 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | **Headline is the method** | 12 | The headline names the system, the rule or the number, sized and placed as the design system says; a reader who sees only the headline learns something. | A topic ("Outbound in 2026"); a question; hero-sized type that pushes content off the canvas. |
| K2 | **Payoff on the canvas** | 14 | The creative delivers the value itself (the steps, the chart, the numbers), not a teaser for the post. Every block has its own mini-visual; 6–9 blocks for an infographic. | "Read the post to find out"; text in boxes; a single quote on a coloured background. |
| K3 | **Every number is real and public** | 14 | Each figure on the creative exists somewhere the reader can reach (a page, a repo, a published case). Counts on the creative equal counts on the landing it points to. | "156 signals" from a private catalog; "63 skills" when the page says 69; a promise the landing does not deliver. |
| K4 | **Brand tokens exact** | 10 | Every colour, font, logo lock-up and footer element matches the design system named in the framing; the accent stays the minority colour; the "positive" colour appears only on genuinely positive outcomes; text on an accent fill keeps contrast. | Off-palette colours; a font the system does not use; a distorted or wrong logo; the footer missing or different from the system. |
| K5 | **Readable in the feed** | 10 | Legible at phone width; non-Latin scripts (e.g. Cyrillic) render in the intended font, not a serif fallback; contrast passes; no block below ~11 px. | Serif Cyrillic; 6-point labels; ten blocks crammed in. |
| K6 | **CTA matches the mechanism** | 10 | Comment-gate keyword, command or "write me", consistent with the post; the promised deliverable exists at the moment of posting. | A CTA that points at a page not yet live; a question as CTA; two CTAs. |

**Type hard fails:** client names or logos; a number with no public backing; a CTA to a
deliverable that does not exist yet; text rendered in a fallback font; colours outside the
design system.

**Judge input:** the rendered creative(s) as images, the design system that applies (one line + token file), the post text if any, the URL of the
landing it points to (the judge opens it or receives its pre-gate claims list), this
rubric, core.md. NOT the brief, NOT the previous versions.
