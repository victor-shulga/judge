# Core rubric — applies to EVERY artifact type (30 of 100 points)

The type rubric (post / article / deck / proposal / landing / site / creative) supplies the
other 70. The judge scores both from ONE read, blind: text or page only, no backstory.

## Core categories

| # | Category | Pts | 10/10 looks like | 0/10 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Promise is backed** | 10 | Every number, claim and "we have X" in the artifact has something behind it that the reader can reach: a link, a file, a section, a source. Counts match across the artifact (the hero says 70, the table sums to 70). | A number with nothing behind it; a promise that sends the reader to a place that does not answer it; two different counts for the same thing. |
| C2 | **Reader gets somewhere** | 10 | The artifact ends in ONE next step the reader can take right now, and the path to it is visible without guessing (button, command, reply instruction). For a page: the path works without a terminal. | No next step; three competing next steps; a step that needs knowledge the reader was never given. |
| C3 | **Voice, not slop** | 10 | Reads like one person who has done this, with specifics. No AI constructions (myth flip, triads, mirrored pairs, reversals, comma upsell, empty run-up), no corporate water, no calqued jargon. | Could have been written about any company by anyone; generic advice; rhetoric instead of observation. |

## Core hard fails (auto-reject regardless of total)

- **Confidentiality:** a client name, client case, client logo or a third-party vendor name in an artifact meant for a public or shareable surface. (The mechanical pre-gate checks a private list; the judge also flags any company that reads like a client.)
- **Invented number:** a figure the artifact cannot back and that is not clearly marked as an estimate.
- **Unbacked promise on a gated page:** the page behind a form promises something (a file, a map, "all X") that the page does not actually deliver.
- **Any construction from the anticopywriting structural pre-gate** in Ukrainian prose (the detector settles these before the judge runs).

## What the judge is NOT told

Why the artifact was made, what the author tried, previous drafts, the creative it came
from, the campaign plan. Blind means the judge reads it the way a stranger in the feed, the
inbox or the browser would.

## Scoring discipline

- Score harshly. 9–10 in a category is rare and means "nothing to fix here".
- When torn between two scores, give the lower one.
- `one_fix` must be one concrete edit ("replace the second bullet with the 437-of-5000 number"),
  never "improve the hook".
- `lowest` is the category with the biggest points gap to its maximum, not the lowest raw score.
